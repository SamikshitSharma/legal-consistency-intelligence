## Module 1: The Interactive Clause Auditor & Risk Dashboard

* What it does: The user uploads a single contract (like a lease or employment agreement).
* How it works: The tool parses the text and displays a visual dashboard with color-coded alerts:
* 🔴 High-Risk Flags: (e.g., “Section 8 allows the landlord to enter without notice. This severely limits your privacy rights.”)
   * 🟡 Hidden Obligations: (e.g., “You are obligated to pay a maintenance fee every 6 months that increases by 5% annually.”)
   * 🟢 Key Milestones: (e.g., “Termination notice must be given exactly 60 days before contract end.”)

## Module 2: The Multi-Contract Comparison Grid

* What it does: The user uploads two or three versions of a document (e.g., their original NDA draft vs. the counter-party’s edited version).
* How it works: The engine displays a side-by-side Markdown Matrix Table. It directly highlights text omissions, changed liability percentages, or shifts in jurisdiction clauses so the user instantly understands what the other side changed.

## Module 3: The "Pre-Counsel" Attorney Briefing Package

* What it does: This hits the exact requirement of "helping users prepare information for a legal professional".
* How it works: After the document is analyzed, the app generates a downloadable, highly structured PDF or Markdown file containing:
* A concise, one-page executive summary of the document.
   * A generated list of 5 targeted questions the user should ask their human lawyer (e.g., "Ask your attorney if the indemnity cap in Section 12 is standard for your industry").
